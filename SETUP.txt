SETUP
=====

grab transfer_server (linux) or transfer_server.exe (windows) and run it

linux:
  chmod +x transfer_server
  ./transfer_server

windows:
  just double click transfer_server.exe or run it in cmd

default port is 5812, port forward that on your router
files get saved to ./received/<code>/


-------------------------------------------------------------------
SENDING FILES - LINUX
-------------------------------------------------------------------

grab transfer (linux client), then:

  chmod +x ./transfer
  ./transfer <server_ip> 5812 <code> <folder or file>

example:
  ./transfer 1.2.3.4 5812 mycode ./world

supports TLS automatically on port 443, or use --tls to force it


-------------------------------------------------------------------
SENDING FILES - WINDOWS
-------------------------------------------------------------------

grab transfer.exe (no TLS) or transfer_tls.exe (TLS / port 443)

  transfer.exe <server_ip> 5812 <code> <folder or file>
  transfer_tls.exe <server_ip> 443 <code> <folder or file>

example:
  transfer.exe 1.2.3.4 5812 mycode world

transfer_tls.exe supports TLS automatically on port 443, or use --tls


-------------------------------------------------------------------
FLAGS (all clients)
-------------------------------------------------------------------

  --nr          dont go into subfolders
  --nj          skip jar files
  --ignore xyz  skip anything with xyz in the name
